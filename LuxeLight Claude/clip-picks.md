## How the user times a clip to the line

These are what separates their edit from a keyword match. Each comes from STOCKING V1 unless noted.

- **The action lands on its word.** The in-point is set so the change happens as the word is said: the light goes on at "switched it on", off at "and it was off", red at "to red light", and the dark garage fills with light across "lit up the whole garage at once".
- **One clip, split to follow a step-by-step sentence.** "One wave by his temple / switched it on / another wave / and it was off" is one clip (`Wave Sensor/588C2E7C…`) used twice: from 2.57s for the wave on, then from 5.33s for the wave off. The dead second between the two actions is cut out, and the second piece starts on the caption change.
- **The feature named for the first time gets a clean close-up demo,** then the story clips follow. "With the wave sensor on" is a hand waving over the light in the dark at 1.9x, before cutting to the man in the garage using it.
- **Feature demos come from the story's setting.** The script puts him in the garage under the car, so every demo in that stretch is a garage or car clip: the wave sensor, the beam lighting the garage, even the phone-torch comparison is under a car hood.
- **A comparison with the old light shows the old light.** "Instead of the one little circle he used to aim with his phone flashlight" is two phone-torch clips under the car hood. This is different from rule 6.45, where the line is about a chore the product removes (fumbling for a button), which you never show.
- **Each item in a list gets its own shot on its word,** around 0.5s each: "chocolate" (Lindor in a stocking), "socks" (Nike socks), "and a gift card" (an Apple card).
- **A person is introduced by who they are.** "Was my dad's, he's 67" is a grey-haired older man with the light on. "Orange went to my brother" is the orange light held in front of a man's hi-vis shirt. The colour comes first, then the person using it.
- **Proof of a payoff shows it still in use later.** "And not a single one forgotten by New Year's" is someone night fishing with it on.
- **Story moments with the narrator's family are AI clips of one consistent cast** (the husband in the green sweater appears in the hook, "he held his up, laughed", and "on the phone to his buddies"). Product and feature beats are always real library clips.
- **Speeds.** AI clips run at 1.1 to 1.2x with a 1.03 zoom. A fast in-hand demo can run at 1.9x. A downloaded clip that's too quick is slowed to 0.5x.
- **Sound.** Real clips where you can hear the room or the product (Colour Swatches, Red Light, Campers, garage clips) keep their sound. Downloaded and AI clips are muted, apart from AI clips with a reaction in them.
